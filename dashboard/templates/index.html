How to read this

Rule IDs come from the detection catalog — DR-1..DR-7 (SPEC.md §8) and GR-1..GR-4 (§20.3) — and each rule carries its own colour in the feed. A correlated mark is DR-7: one source IP in both the honeypot log and the monitor log inside the correlation window.

Where the numbers come from

Every figure comes from the shared SQLite store — nothing here is sample data. The search box and the filters act on the alerts already loaded on this page, not on the whole store.

Reading the timeline

Bars are bucketed per minute in UTC and labelled in this browser's local time; quiet minutes are plotted as zero rather than skipped.
